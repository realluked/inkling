# Inkling

Duolingo-style revision for **Cambridge IGCSE First Language English (0500)**, made by **realluked**.

**Play it here: https://realluked.github.io/inkling/**

- Bite-size lessons for every question on Paper 1 (Reading) and Paper 2 (Writing)
- Writing desk: 22 timed exam tasks and 20 short-answer questions, with mark schemes and model answers
- AI examiner: a mark for each objective using level bands, with written feedback and corrections
- Practice papers: two full Paper 1s and two full Paper 2s in exam conditions, with a 2-hour timer
- Ink check: instant feedback on length, sentence variety, weak words, slang, spelling traps and copied phrases
- Word Hoard: 60+ ambitious words with spaced repetition, plus Word Sprint and Device Dash
- Exam countdown, a daily revision plan, notes, a cheat sheet and an XP league
- Optional account: play as a guest, or log in to sync progress across devices (one account also works on Bitwise)

All reading passages, questions and model answers are original. Not affiliated with Cambridge International.
